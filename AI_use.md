## 2. Overall Description

### 2.1 Users

- Drivers and vehicle owners
- Parking facility operators
- City transportation departments
- System administrators
- Finance and billing personnel
- Third-party service providers

### 2.2 System Environment

The platform will be accessible through supported mobile devices and parking kiosks. An internet connection is required for real-time parking information and integration with external services.

### 2.3 Solution Overview

The Smart Parking Platform provides a centralized system for finding, reserving, and paying for parking. Drivers can view parking availability on an interactive map, make reservations, process digital payments, and receive notifications. Parking operators can monitor occupancy and access reporting tools.

### 2.4 Strengths and Limitations

| Strengths | Limitations |
|---|---|
| Centralizes parking services | Requires internet access |
| Provides real-time parking availability and maps | Depends on accurate parking data |
| Supports reservations and digital payments | Depends on external mapping and payment services |
| Provides operator dashboards and reporting | Physical parking hardware is out of scope |

### 2.5 Competitive Differentiation

The platform combines driver services and parking management in a single system. It supports the parking process from finding and reserving a space to payment and activity tracking, while providing operators with occupancy monitoring and reporting tools.

### 2.6 Constraints and Assumptions

**Constraints:**
- Fixed development timeline
- Dependence on third-party services and APIs
- Compliance with security, privacy, and applicable laws and city ordinances

**Assumptions:**
- Users will have internet access.
- Accurate parking availability data will be provided.
- External mapping and payment services will remain available.

## Note

“Unlimited development budget” was removed from the constraints because an unlimited budget is not normally considered a constraint. If the project specifically requires an unlimited budget, it can instead be listed as an assumption.
